Crew stops

Charlotte Pass is shuttle only. No private cars past Perisher; the shuttle runs 06:00–19:00 Friday and online bookings closed 13 Nov. No ticket means skip CP3 and CP4, and Dan uses his drop bag.

One crew member helps at a time, within about 500 m of the checkpoint. Times follow the scenario chosen above.

Drives: Jindabyne → Bullocks Flat about 25 min. Guthega Base Camp → Guthega Power Station about 30 min incl. a 5–10 min walk (gravel, limited parking). Keller Plaza: shuttle or 900 m walk from Friday Flat. Everyone needs a NSW Parks Pass.

        Race morning

        Thursday 26 Nov

        1. dayCarb-load: rice, pasta, bread, potatoes, juice. Low fibre, no alcohol.
        2. 16:00Lay out kit and check the mandatory list. Charge headlamp, backup light, watch, phone.
        3. 18:00Early dinner: pasta or rice, tomato sauce, tofu. No beans or spice.
        4. 19:00Pack the vest. Mix Tailwind flasks. Alarms for 01:00.
        5. 20:00Lights out.

        Friday 27 Nov (race day)

        1. 01:00Wake up. Glass of water.
        2. 01:10Breakfast, 100–130 g carbs: 2 white bagels with jam and a banana. Coffee if normal.
        3. 01:40Dress: anti-chafe, taping, tattoo dressing if needed. Final kit check.
        4. 02:00Last solids. Sip 300–500 ml water or electrolyte. Toilet.
        5. 02:15Leave for Banjo Paterson Park (street parking).
        6. 02:30Drop finish bag near the start. Toilet.
        7. 02:45One gel. Vest and headlamp on. Watch on Trail Run, 145 bpm alert, GPS locked.
        8. 03:00Start. Under 130 bpm on flats, under 140 on climbs.

        Joel's checklist

          Crew rules

          • Help only at crew checkpoints, one person at a time. No help or running with Dan in between.
          • No crew at CP10 Ngarigo, and no parking on the Alpine Way.
          • Don't eat checkpoint food. Bring your own cup: there are none.
          • No drop bag at a checkpoint where crew will be.
          • Crew breaches can get Dan penalised or disqualified. Follow marshals.
